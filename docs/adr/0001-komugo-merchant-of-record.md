# Komugo is merchant of record

Komugo collects all payments through Billplz (FPX, DuitNow, cards), deducts Commission, and pays Operators on a schedule. We rejected marketplace split payments (fewer Malaysian payment methods) and Operator-collected payments (breaks the one-stop checkout). Consequence: Komugo owns Refunds, chargebacks and SST invoicing, and needs Billplz KYC and a registered business.
