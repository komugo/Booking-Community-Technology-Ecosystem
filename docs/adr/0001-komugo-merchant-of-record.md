# Komugo is merchant of record

Komugo collects all payments through CHIP Collect (FPX, DuitNow QR, cards, e-wallets), deducts Commission, and pays Operators on a schedule. We rejected marketplace split payments (fewer Malaysian payment methods) and Operator-collected payments (breaks the one-stop checkout). We moved from Billplz to CHIP before launch for e-wallets out of the box and a refund API, so Refunds go back automatically and only failures reach staff. Consequence: Komugo owns Refunds, chargebacks and SST invoicing, and needs CHIP KYC and a registered business. Payouts stay manual bank transfers by staff; CHIP Send can automate them later.
