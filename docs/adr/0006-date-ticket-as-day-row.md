# A Date ticket's date is stored as a row in `timeslots`

A Date ticket has no Timeslot for the Customer: it admits one Participant any time during the opening hours (the Activity's, else its Venue's) of one date. But Tickets, Holds, Closures, closure notices, Reschedules, check-in and the D1 projections all hang off a single `timeslot_id`. Rather than add a parallel `ticket_date` path through all of them, the booking engine keeps one `timeslots` row per open date per active Date ticket Option, with `starts_at` = that day's opening time and a new nullable `ends_at` = its closing time. Only Date ticket rows have `ends_at`, so existing rows and code keep working unchanged.

The engine copies the opening hours from D1 (`activities.hours` in the Durable Object) whenever an Activity, its Venue or a Date ticket Option is saved, and regenerates the dates 60 days ahead like a Schedule, moving unbooked dates in place when the hours change and keeping booked ones. Date ticket Options need no Resources: availability is the Daily limit minus issued Tickets and people on active Holds, and unlimited (`spots` = 999) when unset.

Consequences: "sold until the Booking cutoff before closing" reads `coalesce(ends_at, starts_at)`; the free-cancel window and check-in day count from `starts_at` (opening time); a Closure of the row closes the date. The Customer-facing UI and emails say "date" and show the opening hours, never "Timeslot".
