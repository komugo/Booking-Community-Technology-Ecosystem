# Komugo Digital

Komugo's software house: websites, booking systems and custom software for businesses and government, sold from digital.komugo.com.

## Language

**Client**:
A business, organisation or government agency that buys from Komugo Digital.
_Avoid_: Customer, Operator, user

**Package**:
A fixed-scope offer with a one-time development fee and its own Hosting fee: Starter, Business, Corporate, Commerce, Booking system, Enterprise & government. Government packages (landing page, corporate portal, booking portal, custom system) are quoted by proposal only.
_Avoid_: Plan, tier, product

**Booking system**:
The Package that delivers a Client their own booking site, customised to their rules and branding, built on the Komugo Platform booking engine.
_Avoid_: White-label, booking app

**Add-on service**:
Extra scope sold on top of a Package (extra page, branding, translation, payment integration), indicatively priced.
_Avoid_: Add-on (a Komugo Platform term), extra, upsell

**Hosting**:
The annual fee every delivered site pays, priced per Package: servers, DNS, SSL, daily backups kept 30 days, uptime monitoring, security patches and fixes to what Komugo Digital delivered. No content changes.
_Avoid_: Maintenance, care, server fee

**Care plan**:
An optional monthly service on top of Hosting: Essential (RM99, 2 Content changes a month), Business (RM199, 5, plus a quarterly security check), Professional (RM399, 10, plus priority and a monthly analytics report) or Enterprise SLA (from RM1,000, agreed in contract). Unused changes don't roll over; new pages or features are quoted separately.
_Avoid_: Support plan, subscription, retainer

**Content change**:
One edit to text, images or prices on an existing page, taking at most 30 minutes.
_Avoid_: Update, revision, ticket

**Lead**:
A prospective client's enquiry from the site: a quote request (usually naming a Package) or a government proposal request. Kept for staff in the dashboard and alerted to staff on WhatsApp.
_Avoid_: Customer, Booking, ticket, contact

**Proposal request**:
A government or GLC Lead, with agency name, procurement route (direct purchase, quotation, tender) and timeline.
_Avoid_: RFQ, tender

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Add-on" in Komugo Platform is an extra bought with an Option; here it's always **Add-on service**.
- "Maintenance" was used for both the yearly fee and the monthly plans. Resolved: **Hosting** is yearly and required; a **Care plan** is monthly and optional.
- "Customer" belongs to Komugo Platform (a person who pays for a Booking). A business that buys from Komugo Digital is a **Client**.
